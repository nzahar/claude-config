## What did NOT work

**The proposed fix to the size cap was rejected, and the rejection is the finding.** The design was:
measure bytes instead of lines, measure the protected meaning-layer block too, calibrate thresholds
against the 55-file corpus. It was correct on its own terms and still wrong — a bloat meter that
fires accurately does not reduce what gets written. Owner's verdict: «Какие-то танцы бесполезные.»
Do not re-propose a threshold change in any unit.

No tooling failed this session. No commands errored.

## Key decisions

- **Delete the size-cap mechanism rather than repair it.** It spans
  `/root/.claude/lib/doc-compaction-contract.md` (65 lines), D8 in
  `/root/.claude/rules/workflow.md`, and wiring in `document-agent`, `experiment-doc-agent` and
  `code-reviewer`. Across 55 codemaps it flags one file. Owner rejected repairing it; deleting it
  is this session's proposal and is not yet approved.
- **The cap failed for three compounding reasons**, all verified, all recorded here so the next
  session need not re-derive them: it counts lines while content sits in lines up to 54 578 chars;
  it excludes the meaning-layer block, which is 83 % of the worst file; and the D7 cell-trim rule
  relocates surplus prose *into* that excluded block, so compacting a codemap lowers its measured
  size without lowering the file. For `REPORT.md` the same relocation lands in a counted section —
  the asymmetry is codemap-only.
- **The structural defect is that one branch is documented four times**: a plan, usually an ADR, a
  `## Data flow` bullet in the codemap, and a `docs/history.md` entry. The codemap bullet is the
  derived copy — 276 KB of `sim-infra.md`'s 639 KB. This was first stated in the
  `follow_me_camera` handoff named above and remains **unverified as a general claim**; it was
  checked on two sampled bullets only.
- **Two operational lessons from that handoff need one line each, not a mechanism**: do not edit
  files a dispatched agent is writing (it stashed and restored itself mid-run), and do not poll a
  background run with `pgrep -f <pattern>` (it matches the wrapper shell running that very command).

## Next steps

Proposals for the owner to accept or reject, ordered by how much they remove.

1. **Cut the size cap.** Delete `/root/.claude/lib/doc-compaction-contract.md`, D8 from
   `/root/.claude/rules/workflow.md`, and the D8 wiring at `agents/code-reviewer.md:95`,
   `agents/experiment-doc-agent.md:55,166`, `agents/document-agent.md:48,80`. Keep the four
   pass-cost levers (`read-once`, `read-parallel`, `batch-edit`, `scope-strict`) somewhere — they
   are the only part with observable effect.
2. **Decide which artifact owns a branch's story, and make the other three pointers.** Candidate:
   ADR owns *why*, codemap owns *current structure only* with no chronological narrative, plan and
   history.md are deleted at merge. This is the change that stops the growth; everything else
   trims symptoms.
3. **Shrink or retire the plan artifact.** `follow_me_camera` holds 92 plans averaging 28 KB —
   2.8 MB against 1016 KB of codemaps. Nothing ever retires one. Either plans are deleted at merge
   (git keeps them) or the full-track template shrinks hard.
4. **Re-examine the four document agents.** `document-agent`, `experiment-doc-agent`,
   `plan-reviewer` and `handoff-reviewer` are 888 of the framework's 2021 lines. `plan-reviewer` is
   the single largest file at 280 lines and reviews a document read once.
5. **Stop archiving handoffs.** The framework calls a handoff ephemeral, then `pickup-handoff`
   moves it to `docs/handoffs/archive/` permanently — 78 files, 786 KB fleet-wide. Git already
   remembers.
6. **Add the two one-line rules** from § Key decisions to `CLAUDE.md` §Sub-agents and
   §Tool Hygiene, if and only if the rework keeps those sections.
